How RPI is calculated: RPI = (Win % × 25%) + (Opp Win % × 50%) + (Opp-Opp Win % × 25%). Win % is weighted by location: road win = 1.4, home win = 0.6, road loss = 0.6, home loss = 1.4. Opp Win % excludes games against the team being calculated.
