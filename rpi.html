How RPI is calculated: RPI = (Win % × 25%) + (Opp Win % × 50%) + (Opp-Opp Win % × 25%). Win % is weighted by location: road win = 1.4, home win = 0.6, road loss = 0.6, home loss = 1.4. High Rank / Low Rank = best / worst rank this season. Last Week = rank from previous week. Rem SOS (Remaining Strength of Schedule) = the average RPI of a team's remaining regular-season opponents. A higher value indicates a tougher remaining schedule. Opp RPI shows this value and Rem SOS Rank ranks teams by it within their division (1 = toughest remaining schedule).
