The Ground Rules

✅ Share It

Share our photos on your personal social media. Keep the image intact and credit Ball 603 and the photographer.

🚫 Don’t Pass It Along

Personal downloads are for your use only. Don’t send the file to a business, club, AAU program, recruiting service, designer or organization for them to use.

✂️ Keep the Credit

Don’t crop out or cover Ball 603 watermarks or photographer credits when sharing our posted images.

🎨 Edited ≠ Owned

Adding text, logos, filters or graphics doesn’t change who owns the original photo or eliminate the need for proper usage rights.
