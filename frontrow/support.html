FAQs

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. Everything on NEC Front Row is free to watch and there is nothing to sign in to.

The video won't start, or I'm seeing a black screen.

Give it a few seconds first — the player quietly retries on its own a few times before giving up.

If it's still stuck, reload the page. That clears up the large majority of cases. Beyond that, check that other video sites play, and try turning off any ad blocker or VPN for this site, since those can block the video stream.

Which browsers and devices work?

Any current browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox on a computer, Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android. If yours hasn't been updated in a long while, update it — older versions can't play the video.

The video keeps stopping to buffer, or looks blurry.

That's almost always the connection. Quality adjusts automatically to what your internet can carry, so a slower connection gets a softer picture rather than constant stalling.

Closing other streaming devices on the same network, moving closer to the router, or plugging in with a cable all help.

There's no sound.

Browsers often block sound until you interact with the page — click or tap the video once.

In Multiview, only the window you've selected plays sound. Click a different window to move the sound to it.

Can I watch on my TV?

Yes. Use the Watch on TV button in the player for AirPlay on Apple devices and Safari, or Chromecast in Chrome. The button only appears once your device finds a TV on the same network, so if you don't see it, check that the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi.

Can I watch more than one game at once?

Yes — that's Multiview. You can watch up to four games together, side by side, as picture-in-picture, three up or in a quad.

Start from any game page with Add to Multiview, then fill the other windows with + Add a game. Your layout is kept in the address, so you can send someone the link and they'll see the same setup.

How do I find an older game?

Everything from the season lives under On Demand, where you can search or filter by sport, school, year and type. School pages also list that school's full season.

What is My Teams?

Pick the schools and sports you care about and their games move to the front everywhere on the site. It's saved on the device you're using, so there's no account and nothing to log in to — but it won't follow you to a different phone or computer.

Can I save a clip?

Use the clip button in the player to grab the last 10, 20 or 30 seconds. On a phone you can save or share it; on a computer it downloads. Clips are made right in your browser, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Can I add NEC Front Row to my phone's home screen?

Yes. On iPhone or iPad, tap the share button in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install or Add to Home screen. It then opens full screen like an app.

A game I expected to see isn't listed.

Schedules come from the conference and its schools, and start times move — especially for weather. If a game should be there and isn't, or a stream never came up, open a support ticket and tell us which game and what you saw.
