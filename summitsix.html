The Summit Six Pics of the Week

Six of the top images from around Granite State volleyball, every week.

The Summit Six Volleyball Club is a featured partner for the 2026 girls volleyball season and each week, we will feature The Summit Six Pics of the Week… six of the top images from around Granite State volleyball. Whether it’s action shots, celebrations, teamwork or officials, The Summit Six Pics of the Week will shine a spotlight on some of the top moments we capture.

Summit Six Volleyball Club is a New Hampshire-based girls volleyball program focused on developing competitive athletes and creating opportunities to compete at the highest levels of the game. Founded in 2025, Summit Six has quickly established itself as one of the region’s top rising volleyball programs, with teams competing throughout New England and across the country.
